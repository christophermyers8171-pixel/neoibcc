# Cardiology

Neonatal cardiovascular physiology, hemodynamic assessment, and the cardiovascular phenotypes encountered in the NICU. The cluster's center of gravity is **neonatal hemodynamics and acquired cardiovascular dysfunction**.

> [!note] CHD scope
> Deep, lesion-by-lesion CHD management lives in dedicated pediatric cardiology resources and is cross-linked here rather than duplicated. CHD content in this cluster is limited to NICU-facing decisions: recognition, ductal-dependent stabilization, and post-op management.
